Method

    Limitations

    • Synthetic data with known built-in effects; real behaviour is messier.
    • Short horizon: D7/D14 retention and first-week revenue. Long-term value relies on stated multipliers.
    • The purchase and revenue guardrails are underpowered for their limits, so a pass means no evidence of harm, not proof of none.
    • Segment findings are exploratory; iOS skews towards T1 markets, so platform and country effects overlap.
    • Retention uses UTC calendar days; day-0 sessions (the CUPED covariate) are measured after randomisation.
    Simulated data. Every number on this page is generated by the project's pipeline (make all) from outputs/tables/results.json; nothing is typed by hand.
    Full write-ups: docs/01_experiment_plan.md (pre-registration) and docs/02_experiment_readout.md (readout).
